## ATCOSIM

- Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jzuluaga/atcosim_corpus
- Original corpus: https://www.spsc.tugraz.at/databases-and-tools/atcosim-air-traffic-control-simulation-speech-corpus.html

The Hugging Face dataset card states that the licensing status depends on the
legal status established by the original ATCOSIM corpus creators. Review the
original corpus terms before using or redistributing ATCOSIM material.

Metadata and transcripts under `files/UWB_ATCOSIM/` may contain material
derived from ATCOSIM and UWB-ATCC and remain subject to their respective terms.

## ATCO2 One-Hour Test Set

- Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jzuluaga/atco2_corpus_1h
- Project: https://www.atco2.org/

The linked Hugging Face dataset card does not clearly declare a license for the
dataset itself. The MIT license of the companion ATCO2 code repository should
not be assumed to apply to the audio or transcripts. Review the dataset
provider's terms before using or redistributing ATCO2 material.

Metadata and transcripts under `files/ATCO2/` remain subject to the applicable
ATCO2 dataset terms.

## Synthetic Audio

Synthetic audio and its metadata remain subject to the licenses and usage terms
of the source transcripts, source voices, speech-generation systems, and any
other material used to produce them. They are not covered by the repository's
Apache License 2.0.

## Model Weights

Model weights are not covered by the repository's Apache License 2.0 unless
explicitly stated otherwise. Their use and redistribution may also be affected
by the licenses of pretrained models and training datasets.
